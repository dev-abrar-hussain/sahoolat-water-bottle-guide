# Hydration for Runners: How to Choose the Right Water Bottle for Long Runs in Pakistan

Running in Pakistan's heat makes hydration part of the training itself, not an afterthought. A good running bottle should be light enough to carry for an hour, easy to sip from without breaking stride, and sealed well enough that it never leaks into your pocket or running belt. For most runs under an hour, a 500ml bottle is enough. For longer runs in summer, carry 750ml to 1 litre, or plan a refill point along your route. Stainless steel keeps water cool far longer than thin plastic, which matters when the sun is strong, though it adds a little weight. Look for a leak-proof cap you can open with one hand and a shape that fits your hand or your belt's bottle holder. A wide mouth helps with cleaning, because a bottle that smells after a few sweaty runs usually ends up unused. Rinse the bottle after every run and wash it properly at least once a week. If you run early in the morning, fill it the night before and keep it in the fridge so it starts cold.
